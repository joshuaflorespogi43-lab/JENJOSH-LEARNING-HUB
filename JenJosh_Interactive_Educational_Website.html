📖 Learn Zone: Fundamental Movements

Fundamental movements are basic body movements that help us perform many physical activities.

🚶 Locomotor Movements

These movements move the body from one place to another.

Examples: walking, running, jumping, hopping, skipping, galloping.

🧍 Non-Locomotor Movements

These movements are done while staying in one place.

Examples: bending, stretching, twisting, turning, swaying.
